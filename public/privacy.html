GymBuddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

GymBuddy is a training and nutrition log. This page explains what it keeps, where it goes, and how to remove it. GymBuddy never sells your data, never shows ads, and never uses your data for advertising.

What GymBuddy keeps

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with.
  • Training: workouts, sets, reps, weights, notes, your program and settings.
  • Body measurements: bodyweight you log.
  • Nutrition (if you turn it on): the foods you log and your targets.
  • Coach chat: your messages with the in-app coaches, and short notes the coach keeps about you (for example an injury or your equipment). You can see and delete these in Settings.
  • Rest alerts (if you turn them on): a push subscription for your phone.
  • Workout heart rate (if you connect Google Health): for each workout, the average, peak, lowest, your heart rate zones and time in each, and each set's peak and how far your heart rate came down after it. The individual readings are not saved to your account (see below).

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase database, readable only by your own signed-in account, and the app is served by Vercel. A copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline. Data is sent over HTTPS and is encrypted at rest by those providers.

Who else receives data

  • Google Gemini (through Vercel's AI Gateway): when you use a coach, your recent training (including each workout's heart rate summary, if you connected Google Health), bodyweight and food are sent to generate the answer.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: food searches and scanned barcodes, to look up nutrition. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • Apple or Google push services: rest alerts, if you turn them on.
  • Google Health: only if you connect it (see below).

Google Health

If you choose to connect Google Health, GymBuddy:

  • Reads your heart rate during your workouts (from the time each one started to when it finished, plus a minute either side), for workouts in the 30 days before you connect and after, and the heart rate zones Google Health works out for you. It works out each workout's average, peak and time in each zone, and each set's peak and recovery, shows them on the workout and gives them to your coaches. Your account keeps only those numbers. The readings for a workout's heart rate chart are kept on the phone that showed it (the last 40 workouts), never on GymBuddy's servers. It reads no other heart rate or other data.
  • Sends your bodyweight and logged food (calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat) to your Google Health account, from the day you connect.

GymBuddy stores the sign-in token Google issues, on its server, encrypted, only for this. Disconnecting in GymBuddy's Settings, or removing GymBuddy at myaccount.google.com/permissions, stops it and deletes the token. Data already sent stays in Google Health until you delete it there; heart rate summaries stay on your workouts in GymBuddy until you delete the workout or your account.

The use of information received from Google Health API will adhere to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health data is not sold or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, credit or lending decisions, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires it.

Keeping or deleting your data

Settings → Export downloads everything as CSV or JSON. Coach chats are deleted after 30 days, and you can clear them sooner in Settings. To delete your account and all of its data, ask the developer by email; it is deleted from GymBuddy's database, including any Google Health token.

Changes
